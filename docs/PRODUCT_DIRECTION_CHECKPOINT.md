# Product Direction Checkpoint

**Checkpoint date:** 2026-09-03  
**Status:** Active validation axis, not an implementation specification  
**Return point:** Reassess after the validation gate below is reached or after 14 days, whichever comes first.

## Root objective

Determine whether Trajecta can become a small, sustainable product by solving
verified work-continuity failures between cloud planning surfaces and local
coding agents.

This checkpoint preserves the reason for the current direction. It does not
make the direction permanent, authorize product claims, or override newer user
input and workspace evidence.

## Reference axis

- Preserve the trajectory, not the transcript.
- Carry material work state: objective, accepted decisions, branch, evidence,
  blockers, open loops, exact next action, provenance, and resume revision.
- Treat transfer packets as candidate context. Current user input and current
  workspace state remain authoritative.
- Prevent stale or ambiguous continuation instead of promising perfect memory.
- Sell concrete continuity outcomes while keeping the product local-first,
  inspectable, bounded, and humane.

## Current settlement

**Selected validation branch:** AI-native solo builders and technical founders
who plan in ChatGPT or Claude and implement in Codex, Claude Code, or Cursor.

**Commercial wedge:** Verified work handoffs across surfaces. The buyer should
be able to stop in one agent and resume in another with the correct branch,
evidence, open loops, and next action.

**Brand position:** Technical buyer, humane voice. Avoid both hard-edged
"tech-bro" performance and vague companion-memory promises.

**Parked branch:** Consumer companion continuity. Keep it available as a later
research or lead-magnet lane, but do not treat Threadkeeper engagement as proof
that buyers will pay for Trajecta.

## Evidence state

### Facts

- The repository currently ships an alpha kernel with work lifecycle, cue
  routing, immutable contract anchors, bounded transfer packets, and a tested
  cloud-to-local round trip.
- Product adapters and the separately governed slow-learning layer are not yet
  shipped.
- Major AI platforms now provide increasingly capable built-in memory,
  projects, and memory import/export features.
- Paid and open-source cross-model memory products already exist.
- Neither Trajecta nor Threadkeeper currently has meaningful public traction.

### Inferences

- Generic "portable AI memory" is becoming commoditized.
- A stale or lossy work handoff has a clearer cost for builders than a manually
  authored continuity passport has for a broad consumer audience.
- Trajecta's strongest distinction is bounded, provenance-bearing work-state
  transfer with exact resume protection, not long-term semantic recall.

### Hypotheses to test

- The first useful customer is a solo builder who switches AI surfaces at
  least twice per week.
- A visible failed-handoff versus verified-resume demo will communicate value
  better than architecture documentation.
- Buyers may pay USD 19 once for a documented starter kit, or USD 19 per month
  when working adapters remove repeated handoff labor.
- GitHub, X, Reddit, Show HN, and developer video channels will outperform
  TikTok and Facebook for the initial builder offer.

### Unknowns

- How often the target user experiences a costly handoff failure.
- Whether a structured Markdown handoff is already sufficient for them.
- Whether installation and adapter maintenance create more friction than the
  product removes.
- Whether willingness to pay attaches to the kernel, adapters, hosted relay,
  onboarding, or support.

## Validation gate

Before buying a domain, running paid ads, or expanding the kernel:

1. Show one truthful end-to-end demo to at least 10 qualified users.
2. Have at least 5 complete one real cross-surface handoff.
3. Observe at least 3 use Trajecta for a second handoff without prompting.
4. Obtain at least 2 paid commitments at the proposed price.

### Continue

Continue toward a product specification when all four conditions are met and
the repeated use depends on Trajecta's revision, branch, or provenance behavior.

### Revise

Revise the offer when users value the workflow but reject the packaging,
installation, or price.

### Park

Keep Trajecta as open infrastructure when users consistently say a simple
`HANDOFF.md` is sufficient, cannot identify a costly failure, or do not repeat
the workflow.

## Next action

Produce one minimal, auditable demonstration:

1. Plan a bounded task in a cloud surface.
2. Hand it to a local coding agent through Trajecta.
3. Create a competing stale or ambiguous handoff.
4. Show Trajecta rejecting or exposing the stale resume.
5. Finish from the correct branch and return a verified outcome.

Do not add product scope until this demonstration produces user evidence.
