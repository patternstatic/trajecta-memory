    A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

    Before you
    pick up the thread.

    Does this connect my AI accounts?+

    The current beta uses JSON files that you transfer yourself. The browser playground uses sample data. Automatic account connections are not included.

    Why not just use a HANDOFF.md?+

    A note may be enough for your workflow. Trajecta helps when an outdated handoff or a repeated resume could change the wrong work: it checks the revision and branch and records what was accepted.

    Where does my work live?+

    The SDK keeps state in your local workspace. This playground runs entirely in your browser with sample data and does not send it to a server.

    What does a successful receipt prove?+

    It proves the bounded local state transition was accepted. The next action still needs to be carried out by you or an agent; a receipt does not establish that the downstream task was completed.
