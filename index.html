The narrow job

Continuity that knows where to resume.

Trajecta is not a new chatbot memory. It is a transport-neutral alpha kernel for preserving material work deltas and moving one exact packet across surfaces.

01

Capture the delta

Record the decision, evidence, blocker, or outcome that changes the work.

02

Select by cue

Route to the right work item and branch without loading every transcript.

03

Resume with proof

Compare revisions, accept the packet, and expose the exact next action.
